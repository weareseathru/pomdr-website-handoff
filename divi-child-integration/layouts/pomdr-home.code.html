Our Mission

Who will care for your dog if you no longer can?

We are a resource and advocate for senior dogs and senior people on California's Central Coast. Through rescue, foster, adoption, hospice, and lifelong care, we make sure that when life changes, no dog is left without someone to love them.

Our Impact

The lives behind the numbers.

Every figure here is really a dog who found home, and the people who got them there. Here are four, with the stories behind them.

3,200+
Senior dogs rescued
Since 2009 across the Central Coast

Cooper came to us at ~13 years old, a surrender from a family moving into memory care. He spent nine weeks in foster and was adopted the same week his profile went live. He lives in Carmel now, going on slow morning walks with his new person. He is one of 3,200.

16yrs
Of lifetime commitment
To every dog that comes into our care

Our commitment has never changed: every dog who comes into our care is here for as long as it takes. Some stay a week. Some stay two years. A few stay forever, in our sanctuary program. The number changes. The promise does not.

1,800+
Active volunteers
Fostering, walking, transporting and caring

On any given Tuesday morning, a dozen volunteers are walking dogs before their own workday starts. They are retired teachers, college students, remote workers, weekend-only folks. They show up. That is everything.

501c3
Tax-deductible giving
EIN 27-1154816, founded October 2009

Every dollar given to POMDR goes directly toward food, medical care, foster reimbursement, and the small daily kindnesses that add up to a good life for a senior dog. Hazel went home in 2024 after a three-month stay that included two surgeries. She is doing great.
