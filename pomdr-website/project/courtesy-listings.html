Courtesy Listings

Dogs we list as a courtesy.

These dogs are not in POMDR's care, but their families need help finding the right next home. Adoption goes directly through the current owner.

How courtesy listings work

A courtesy listing is a senior dog whose person can no longer keep them, but who is staying in their current home until adoption. POMDR posts the listing and screens inquiries, but the dog never enters our care. The contract is between you and the current family.

Courtesy dogs appear in the main listing with a Courtesy Listing badge and a category filter on /adopt/.
