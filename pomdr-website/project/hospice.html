Dogs who need a soft landing.

Hospice dogs have weeks or months left, not years. POMDR covers vet care, food, and supplies. You give them a couch, a warm bed, and your presence.

How hospice fostering works

When a dog enters our care with a terminal diagnosis or severe age-related decline, we place them with a hospice foster. The foster commits to comfort, dignity, and presence. POMDR handles the medical and financial side.

  • All vet visits and medications covered
  • Food, supplies, and end-of-life services covered
  • Coordinator available for questions, day or night
  • Grief support and time to step back when needed
