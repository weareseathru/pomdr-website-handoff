Clove

~8 years  ·  Male  ·  15 lb  ·  Chihuahua mix

Foster needed May 20 to Jun 30, current foster traveling Volunteer

Clove came to us from a county shelter where his time was running short. He needed a quiet space to decompress and a person who understood that small dogs grieve too. Today, after a few weeks of foster, he is a different dog: gentle, attentive, and happiest pressed against a warm leg. Good with respectful older kids and other small dogs. Cats unknown.
