Helping Paw Program

Keeping pets and people together.

The Helping Paw Program provides support to senior people and people with disabilities who need help caring for their dogs, so no one has to give up their companion due to hardship.

Our Services

Three ways we help.

Whether you need someone to walk your dog, help with vet bills, or temporary care during a health crisis, Helping Paw is here.

Walking Brigade

Our volunteers walk dogs for senior or ill guardians who can no longer walk their own dogs. We also help with transportation to vets and groomers, and other supportive tasks.

Request a Walker

Financial Assistance

We offer two funds to help guardians facing unexpected veterinary expenses. Max's Helping Paws Fund for urgent care, and a Food Fund to keep dogs fed during financial hardship.

Temporary Foster

When a guardian faces a health crisis or hospitalization, we can arrange temporary foster care for their dog, keeping them safe until their person recovers.

Financial Assistance

Two funds. One mission.

Our financial assistance programs help low-income guardians facing unexpected expenses so they never have to choose between their dog and their budget.

Max's Helping Paws Fund

Provides financial assistance to low-income guardians facing unexpected, urgent veterinary care, including surgery and specialist visits. Named in honor of Max, a beloved POMDR pup.

For urgent, unexpected veterinary needs · Low-income guardians · Monterey, Santa Cruz & San Benito counties
Apply for Max's Fund

Food Fund

Helps guardians who are struggling financially to continue feeding their dogs during times of hardship, keeping families together when budgets are tight.

For food assistance · Must be facing financial hardship · Tri-county area
Apply for Food Assistance

Helping Paw Veterinary Fund

For non-emergency but necessary veterinary care that a guardian cannot afford. Routine treatments, dental care, and ongoing medical management for chronic conditions.

For non-emergency care · Income verification required

Who Qualifies

Are you eligible?

The Helping Paw Program is designed for people in our tri-county area who need support caring for their dog due to age, disability, or financial hardship.

We review each application individually and do our best to help as many people as we can. If we are unable to help, we provide referrals to other resources.

Apply Online
Or call (831) 718-9122
  • Senior or person with disability living in Monterey, Santa Cruz or San Benito county
  • Facing financial hardship that makes caring for your dog difficult
  • Temporarily unable to walk or care for your dog due to health issues
  • Facing unexpected veterinary expenses you cannot cover
  • In need of temporary foster care during hospitalization or recovery
  • Need help with food or basic supplies for your dog
