# The fine-tooth comb: every sentence on the new site against the live site's facts

Done 2026-10-26, after Carie's note that the new site "needs a fine tooth comb"
and Andrew's answers to the live site's nine self-contradictions (recorded in
`docs/POMDR-FACTS-FROM-LIVE-SITE-2026-10-26.md`, section 15). Every page of the
new site was read in full, including the shared strip, header, menu and footer.

Two piles came out of it:

- **Part A, applied now.** Facts that were wrong or unsupported against the
  live site, where Andrew's decisions settled the answer. Fifty replacements on
  22 files, pushed to Ronnie's main (the commit is named in Part D).
- **Part B, for Carie.** Sentences that are grand, invented or promise more
  than the live site promises. Each has the current line and a proposed
  replacement, so she can approve, reword or strike. Nothing in Part B changed
  on the site yet.

The test site: https://pomdr-public.pages.dev/ (rebuilds hourly at 17 past).

---

## Part A. Applied now

### Numbers and labels, site-wide
- Volunteers: 1,500 became **1,800** on the home page, About (twice), Donate,
  Volunteer (twice), the team page and Happy tails.
- "Senior dogs adopted" became **"Dogs adopted"** beside the 4,500+ figure on
  the home page, About, Donate, Volunteer and Happy tails. The count stays.
  The label asserted that every dog is a senior; the live site says most are.
- About: "a staff of twenty-seven" became **nineteen** (the 27 on the team
  page includes the five board members and three advisors). "Most with a
  POMDR dog of their own" became "many".

### Helping Paw page
- "a vet bill up to $500" (three places) became **up to $750, and up to
  $2,750 for major medical emergencies**.
- Eligibility "Mainly for Monterey County residents, with limited funding for
  Santa Cruz and San Benito... social security or disability, unemployed, or
  at or below poverty level" (two places) became the live site's rule:
  **residents of Monterey, Santa Cruz and San Benito counties who can
  demonstrate financial need**.
- "We do not cover microchipping, routine exams or routine dentals... can mail
  it to you" became **routine veterinary care is not covered; flea treatment by
  pre-arrangement, picked up in Pacific Grove**, plus the **spay or neuter
  requirement** the live site states and the new site had dropped.
- "Dog food (we rarely have cat food)" became **"Dog and cat food"**.
- Heading "What we can bring you" became **"Supplies we can give you"**,
  because the paragraph under it says they are picked up.

### Helping Paw application, English and Spanish
- "Who this is for: people in Monterey County... their dog... Nobody is going
  to make you justify it" became the live facts: **three counties, dogs and
  cats, seniors, hospice patients and others in hardship, the $750 and $2,750
  limits, and financial need must be shown**. The Spanish page says the same.

### Benefit Shop
- Hours **Tuesday to Saturday, 11:00am to 5:00pm. Closed Sunday and Monday.**
  The shop's own phone, (831) 312-8991, added.

### Donate
- Sponsor an Ad: $120 became **$150**.

### Fostering and the applications
- Volunteer application card "We pay for the food and the vet" became **"We
  cover the vet care and the supplies; you provide the food."**
- Fostering FAQ "Where does a dog wait until a foster home opens? In a doggy
  suite at our clinic" **removed** (decision: every dog lives in a foster home).
- The food answer on the Fostering page already said "You do... Donated food
  is shared out when we have it", which matches Andrew's answer. Unchanged.

### About
- "Sometimes, when we bring a senior dog into our care, it becomes evident that
  they should not have to endure one more move or life change. Helping Paw is
  how." The first sentence is the live site's reason a dog stays in foster care
  for life; it has nothing to do with Helping Paw. Replaced with the live
  site's own Helping Paw sentence: **"We also provide volunteer and financial
  assistance so seniors can keep their pets with them as long as possible."**
- Timeline 2011: "our office and adoption center" became **"our offices and
  headquarters"**.
- Stat note "Every one placed from a foster home, with a home check and a
  follow-up call every year" became **"Placed from foster homes, with a home
  check and annual follow-up calls."**

### The shared "You have our word" block (About and Donate)
- "Every dog gets a full exam, a senior blood panel and the dental work they
  need before they go home" overstated the live site. Now: **"Every dog gets a
  thorough veterinary exam. Senior dogs also get a senior blood panel and, in
  many cases, a dental cleaning and any extractions they need."**

### Hospice
- "Their medical or behavioral needs mean these dogs stay... Through our
  Perpetual Care promise" was wrong twice: hospice is medical, and Perpetual
  Care is a different program. Now the live site's definition: **a
  life-threatening condition, a few months expected, hospice care in a POMDR
  foster home, all medical expenses covered.**

### Get help and Resources
- "Low-cost veterinary care, food assistance, behavior help and end-of-life
  support" described things the Resources page does not list. Now: **"Financial
  help with vet bills, pet trusts, grief support, daily check-in services and
  products for older dogs, from organizations we trust."**
- "Clarks Law Office" became **Clark Ruggiero Law Office**, with the live link.

### How adopting works, and the questionnaire page
- "Most adoptions take about a week" **removed**; the live site gives no
  timeline.
- Next steps: "by email or phone, whichever you told us you prefer" became
  **by email, check your spam folder**; "At their foster home" became the live
  site's **talk with the foster, then a meet and greet**; "we stay in touch for
  the rest of their life, for advice, for the vet" became **an initial home
  check and annual follow-up calls**.

### Perpetual Care
- "we will come and meet them... none of them rules a dog out on its own"
  became **"we will arrange to meet them. Eligibility depends on your dog's
  age, size, temperament and any special medical needs."**
- "Many people who enroll a dog also remember POMDR in their will" became
  **"You can also remember POMDR in your will or trust"**.

### Help placing your dog
- Step 1 "or call and we will fill it in with you" became the live site's
  **"If you need help filling it out, call...; please be aware that this will
  delay the processing of your request."**

### The Bauer Center
- "our office and our adoption center, where... visiting families come
  together" became **"our offices and headquarters, where staff and volunteers
  do the day-to-day work"**.
- "Adoptable dogs are seen by appointment, so please call ahead" became **"Our
  dogs live in foster homes and are met by appointment, which starts with the
  adoption questionnaire."**
- Card "Adoption center: Come spend time with our dogs" became **"Where
  adoptions begin: Our dogs live in foster homes, not here..."**
- "Volunteers check in here before walks, events and shifts" became **the
  front desk is staffed by volunteers, and the Behavior and Enrichment Team
  runs its shifts with the dogs here** (both on the live site).

### Work with us
- "a handful of people" became **"a small team of about twenty"**.
- The relief veterinarian hours now match the live posting: **temporary relief
  while a vet is on maternity leave, Mondays and every other Thursday from May
  2026.**

## Part B. For Carie: grand, invented, or promising more than the live site does

Each line: where, what it says now, why it is flagged, and a proposed
replacement built from the live site's own words where one exists.

### Home page
1. Slide 2 lead: "Walking, vet rides, financial assistance, and temporary
   fosters, so guardians and their dogs never have to say goodbye too soon."
   *Grand ending.* Proposed: "Walking, rides to the vet, help with vet bills
   and short-term foster care, so people can keep their pets with them as long
   as possible."
2. Slide 2 tag: "Support for senior guardians." *Helping Paw also serves
   hospice patients and others, dogs and cats.* Proposed: "Support for people
   and their pets."
3. Slide 3 lead: "Every dog in our care is ours for life. If a placement does
   not work, for any reason, ever, they come home to us." *The take-back
   promise is not stated on the live site.* Proposed: "We have a lifetime
   commitment to all the dogs who come into our care. They will either be
   adopted into a wonderful, permanent home, or will live out their lives in
   one of our foster homes."
4. Intake card: "We meet each dog where they are, find the right foster home,
   and match them with a person for the rest of their days." *Grand.*
   Proposed: "We find loving foster and forever homes for dogs whose guardians
   can no longer care for them and for senior dogs in shelters."
5. Perpetual Care card: "Plan ahead with us, and we promise to take your dog in
   and find them a home if you no longer can. It costs nothing in your
   lifetime." *Close, but "find them a home" skips the foster-for-life
   outcome.* Proposed: "We make pre-arrangements to take in dogs should their
   guardians become unable to care for them. There is no cost to you during
   your lifetime."
6. Newsletter: home says "One thoughtful email a month... One email a month";
   the mailing-list page says "A few times a month". *Contradiction; the live
   site states no frequency.* Carie picks one.

### About
7. "What we believe: Kindness first, to dogs and to people. It is the POMDR
   culture, and the first thing we ask of anyone who joins us." *The POMDR
   Culture page lists seven commitments; kindness is not one of them by name.*
   Proposed: use the real seven (Feedback, Best Practices, Positive Attitude,
   Solution-driven, Responsibility, Professional, Volunteer and Donor
   Appreciation), or drop the line.
8. "Honesty about what we can do. We say what a dog needs, and what we cannot
   take on." *Invented value, though consistent with the surrender page.* Keep
   or strike.
9. "You have our word" (About and Donate): "We will not put a dog down for
   being old." "If an adoption does not work out, for any reason, ever, the
   dog comes back to us." "You can ask how your dog is doing, for the rest of
   their life." *None of the three is stated on the live site. All three may be
   true; Carie must confirm each before the site promises it.* The same three
   appear on the intake questionnaire page under "What we can promise".
10. Impact reports list shows 2024, 2023, 2022 and 990s for 2023, 2022, 2021.
    The live site has the **2025 Impact Report** and the **2024 990**. Needs the
    PDFs added (Ronnie or Andrew).

### Clinic
11. The whole page reads as a public veterinary clinic: "Quality care, kept
    affordable", "accessible, compassionate veterinary care for our rescue dogs
    and for the senior people and dogs we serve", "By appointment. Please call
    first", "To make an appointment or ask a question", "A question about your
    dog's care? We are here." *The live site says the clinic "provides medical
    care for the dogs in our program" and never offers appointments to the
    public.* Question for Carie: does the clinic see Helping Paw clients' pets
    at all? If not, the page should say plainly that it cares for POMDR's own
    dogs. The footer and Contact page say "By appointment only" for the clinic
    and should change with it.
12. "A clinic of our own, so no dog is turned away" and "we never have to turn
    a dog away because care felt out of reach." *Grand and not on the live
    site; the surrender page lists conditions POMDR cannot take.* Proposed:
    cut, or "A clinic of our own means every dog in our program gets a full
    exam, a senior blood panel, heartworm test, urinalysis, vaccines and a
    microchip."
13. "treating conditions other shelters often cannot take on" and "keeping
    costs manageable so more dogs can heal and more families can stay
    together." *Grand.* Proposed: cut.

### Contact
14. "A real person will answer." *The front desk is staffed by volunteers 11 to
    4; the live site makes no such promise.* Proposed: "Call or email us with
    any question about adopting, fostering, getting help with your dog, or
    giving."
15. "Send checks here, not to the office" under the P.O. Box. *Instruction not
    on the live site.* Keep if true.
16. info@pomdr.org is used site-wide; the live site uses
    info@peaceofminddogrescue.org (the live shop page does use shop@pomdr.org).
    Confirm the short address is monitored.

### Donate
17. "POMDR takes in senior dogs nobody else will, and pays for the dentistry,
    the medication and the surgery that follows. A gift of any size goes
    straight into that." *Grand, and "straight into" implies restriction.*
    Proposed: "Our biggest expense is medical care for the dogs we rescue. A
    gift of any size helps."
18. Monthly: "It is what lets us say yes to the next dog before the money is
    in." *Invented.* Proposed: "Become a sustaining donor and pledge a
    recurring monthly donation. Your card is charged on the first of every
    month."
19. "Every purchase pays for the care of a senior dog" (Benefit Shop, twice).
    Proposed, from the live site: "Shop and support senior dogs and senior
    people."

### Why a senior dog
20. "Peace of Mind Dog Rescue exists for the dogs other shelters overlook" and
    "We changed that math for our community, one dog at a time." *Grand.*
    Proposed: the live page's own opening: "There are so many great reasons to
    adopt an older dog..."
21. "Dogs who have waited the longest tend to bond the deepest." and "They bond
    quickly and fully. A settled dog often attaches faster than a distracted
    puppy." *Claims with no source.* Proposed: the live page's "A senior dog's
    gratitude, love and loyalty is like no other."
22. "We never leave an adopter to navigate care alone" (twice). *Promise not
    on the live site.* Proposed: cut, or "We share each dog's health honestly,
    so you choose with open eyes."
23. "An adoption that does not work out is not held against anyone." Same
    take-back question as item 9.

### Help placing your dog
24. "Some of the dogs we have loved most came to us this way" and "so that the
    next home is the right one and the goodbye is the only hard part." *Grand.*
    Proposed: the live page's "There are times in our lives when we have to
    make self-care a priority... It can be one of the most difficult but also
    most loving and selfless decisions you may need to make. POMDR is here to
    help."

### Perpetual Care
25. "It is the worry that keeps a lot of older owners awake." and "that your
    dog will never wake up alone in a shelter wondering where you went." *Grand.*
    Proposed: the live page's "you can have peace of mind that your dog will
    not end up alone and frightened in an animal shelter."

### Mailing list
26. "We do not sell or share your address, and we will not use it to ask you
    for money every week." *Promise not on the live site.* Keep if true.
27. "what is on at the Benefit Shop" as newsletter content. Confirm.

### Volunteer application
28. "You do not need experience with dogs for most of what we need doing.
    Driving, laundry, the shop and the phones all matter as much as the
    walking." *Invented detail.* Keep if true.

### Adoption questionnaire and foster application
29. The "What we need from you" bullets (vet's name, landlord's name and
    number, two references, hours the dog would be alone) describe the LGL
    form's fields. Carie or whoever owns the LGL forms should confirm they
    match the questions actually asked.

### In the media
30. The live list has two 2026 items the new page lacks: KSBW, "Yappy Hour
    returns to Carmel's Cypress Inn" (Aug 27, 2026) and Pet Care Pointers,
    "Fostering Saves Lives" (Jun 16, 2026). Content add.

### Happy tails
31. Lead "Older dogs who waited, and then did not have to wait any more."
    Proposed: "Dogs who waited, and then did not have to wait any more."
