### Resources
We have compiled some resources for senior dogs and senior people. We hope you find this information helpful.

#### Books
[link: When Your Pet Outlives You: Protecting Animal Companions After You Die -> https://www.amazon.com/When-Your-Pet-Outlives-You/dp/0939165449]
When Your Pet Outlives You is an easy-to-use resource guide designed to help pet owners deal with one of their major, overriding fears: If they die tomorrow, what will happen to their beloved companion animals? This is the first book to address this issue at length, combining personal stories with step-by-step information on how to prepare today, along with examples of legal documents, resources, and an index.
[link: All My Children Wear Fur Coats: How to Leave a Legacy for Your Dog -> http://www.legacyforyourpet.com/]
All My Children Wear Fur Coats is about leaving a legacy for your pet and is a pet estate planning book. The Hall Mark of True Love is planning for your pet's future without you and for your future without your pet.
[link: Perpetual Care: Who Will Look After Your Dogs if You’re Not Around? -> https://www.amazon.com/PerPETual-Care-after-Youre-Around/dp/0965250288]
In this groundbreaking book, you’ll learn the pros and cons of setting up a pet trust, how to select a permanent caretaker, and how to come up with a plan that makes everybody—including your pets—happy.

#### Check-in Services
[link: Iamfine -> http://info.iamfine.com]
Iamfine is a daily check-in service to confirm that senior citizens living alone are OK. If you live alone and have pets, this service could save their life or yours. You set a time to be called each day. Iamfine calls you and you press “1” on your phone. You are now checked in for the day.
[link: Snug -> https://www.snugsafe.com/]
Snug is a free app that checks in daily to make sure you’re okay - and will let someone know if you are not.

#### Financial Assistance for Pet Guardians
[link: Assistance Dog Special Allowance Program -> https://www.cdss.ca.gov/assistance-dogs]
Provides a monthly payment of $50 to eligible persons who use a guide, signal, or service dog to help them with their disability-related needs.
[link: BirchBark Foundation -> https://www.birchbarkfoundation.org]
Provides financial assistance to pet guardians needing assistance with veterinary expenses. Serving Monterey and Santa Cruz Counties.
[link: BluePearl Cares -> https://bluepearlvet.com/bluepearl-cares/]
Exclusive to BluePearl clients. Surgery must be done through BluePearl.
[link: Brown Dog Foundation -> https://www.browndogfoundation.org/ask-for-help]
ExclusiveYou must apply online and a pre-screening application. if you qualify, a case manager will reach out and start the process. They do not work with Blue Pearl Vet Services anymore.
[link: CareCredit -> https://www.carecredit.com]
The CareCredit credit card is a simple, flexible way to pay for veterinary care for your pets. See if you prequalify without impacting your credit score.
[link: Free Animal Doctor -> https://freeanimaldoctor.org/]
Crowdfund individual cases for pets needing expensive procedures, pay directly to veterinarian.
[link: Magic Bullet Cure -> https://themagicbulletfund.org/apply/]
Only for pets who are diagnosed with cancer who will be having surgery and need chemotherapy (funds can only be used for surgery or chemo). This org does not give grants but with guardian participation, they help crowdfund.
[link: The Mosby Foundation -> https://themosbyfoundation.org/apply-for-aid/]
Provides financial assistance for critically sick and injured dogs.
[link: Paws 4 A Cure -> https://www.paws4acure.org/askforhelp.php]
Only help with medications, insulin, heart worm treatment and medical equipment (like doggy wheelchairs). Maximum amount of assistance is $500 and it is a one-time grant.
[link: The Pet Fund -> https://www.thepetfund.com/about-us]
A national nonprofit dedicated to funding veterinary care for those who could not afford it.
[link: Pets Find Help -> https://pets.findhelp.com/]
Search engine for resources using zip code (includes food pantry, financial assistance, pet friendly housing, respite care, etc).
[link: Pets of the Homeless -> https://petsofthehomeless.org/]
Financial assistance for vet care and pet food to unhoused individuals. Must call and do application over the phone.
[link: SAGE Compassion for Animals -> https://www.sagec4a.org/apply/]
Provides financial assistance to pet guardians seeking veterinary care for immediate life-threatening injuries or illnesses. They do not cover initial exam fees or diagnostics. Serving Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano and Sonoma counties in California.
[link: Santa Cruz SPCA -> https://spcasc.org/dr-jeans-senior-friends/]
Financial assistance (grants up to $500, no dentals) for low-income seniors with dogs over six years old living in Santa Cruz County. Also sponsors two annual wellness days for free preventative care.
[link: Saving Gracie -> https://www.saving-gracie.org/angel-fund-apply-for-help/]
Diagnostics must be complete, estimate must be ready, pet must have a good prognosis with no pre-existing conditions, must have exhausted every resource including savings and credit cards.
[link: Second Chances for Blind Dogs -> https://secondchancesforblinddogs.org/application-for-cocos-eye-care-grant]
Provides financial assistance for low-income guardians who have dogs who are blind, have impaired sight or are needing eye care including enucleations, cherry eye procedures and eye surgeries.
[link: Waggle -> https://www.waggle.org/]
Crowdfund up to $2,000 per case, pay directly to veterinarian.

#### Senior Cat Rescues
[link: Golden Oldies Cat Rescue -> http://www.gocatrescue.org]
Golden Oldies Cat Rescue is a resource and advocate for older cats in Monterey County. They re-home cats who are at risk of euthanasia in shelters or whose guardians can no longer care for them. They place these cats, who have no other options of a safe place to go, into temporary foster care. Then find them permanent loving homes, enriching the lives of both the cats and their new families.
They promise a lifetime commitment to the cats in their care. They either will be adopted or will live out their lives in a foster homes.
Golden Oldies is led by a board who share a vision of helping older cats in Monterey County live out their remaining years in safe, secure and loving homes. They are all volunteer and 100 percent foster-based.

#### Senior Dog Rescues
[link: Muttville -> http://www.muttville.org/]
Muttville is a non-profit, 501(c)3 organization, based in San Francisco, CA dedicated to improving the lives of senior dogs. On a local level, Muttville rescues senior dogs and finds them new homes or gives them hospice. On a global level, Muttville provides information about caring for older dogs and support for people who do.
[link: Old Dog Haven -> http://www.olddoghaven.org/]
OldDog Haven is a small nonprofit group, based in the state of Washington, using a large network of people to provide a loving safe home for abandoned senior dogs. Our goal is to provide a loving, safe home for senior dogs abandoned at this stage of their lives. When we have room, and the means, we take these dogs into our homes; if possible we adopt out those with a reasonable life-expectancy. We care for the rest as long as they have good quality of life. In addition, we try to assist owners in finding new homes for their senior dogs through our website and referrals.
[link: Senior Dog Project -> http://www.srdogs.com/]
The Senior Dogs Project site presents a broad listing of various agencies that help to rehome senior dogs (over the age of five). You can find a dog to adopt or seek help in placing a dog through these agencies.
[link: Tails of Gray -> http://www.tailsofgray.org]
Tails of Gray is dedicated to saving the lives of innocent senior dogs left homeless for any reason, providing quality medical care and placing them into loving, responsible and permanent homes whenever possible.
