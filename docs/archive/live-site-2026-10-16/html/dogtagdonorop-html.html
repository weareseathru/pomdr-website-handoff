Dog Tag Donor

Your sponsorship will help POMDR carry out our mission of helping senior dogs and senior people.

Dog Tag Donor: $500

Your name will be displayed on a dog tag on our Dog Tag Donor Board at our POMDR Boand Clinic. The dog tag donation will help cover the cost of veterinary care for the dogs we rescue. There is room to include the name of a person or dog you are honoring as well.
