Donate to Peace of Mind Dog Rescue

We appreciate your donation of any size. POMDR relies heavily on donations from the public to further our mission. Our biggest expense is medical care for the dogs we rescue.

Peace of Mind Dog Rescue is a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization, Tax ID #27-1154816. Your donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowable by law.

Or choose one of these other ways to give:
Max's Helping Paws Fund
To provide financial assistance to low income pet guardians needing unexpected, urgent or emergent veterinary care including surgery and specialist visits. More info.
Benefit Shop
Visit the POMDR Benefit Shop at 223 Grand Ave, Suite 1 in Pacific Grove. It's where hidden gems are found. Shop and support senior dogs and senior people.
Monthly Donation
Become a sustaining donor and pledge a recurring monthly donation. Your credit card will be charged your pledged amount at the first of every month.
Tribute Donation
Make a donation in honor of or in memory of a special person or animal. We will send a special tribute card announcing your gift.
Sponsor a Dog
Sponsor one of our adoptable dogs and help provide care. Go to our adoptable dog page, choose a dog, and click on the "Sponsor" button.
Helping Paw
Make a donation to our Food Fund or Helping Paw Veterinary Assistance Fund to keep pet guardians and their pets together.
Monica Rua Silver Hearts Fund
For dogs with dire medical needs including special surgeries, specialist visits, physical rehabilitation, supplements and medications. Named in honor of co-founder Monica Rua. More info.
Sponsor an Ad
Placing ads in local newspapers helps us find homes for more dogs left behind. These ads are very successful in placing our dogs. Ad sponsorships are $120 per ad.
Dog Tag Donor
Become a Dog Tag Donor at the Boand Clinic. Your sponsorship can be in memory of or in honor of a person or animal.
Sponsor a Doggie Suite
Sponsor a doggie suite at the Boand Clinic for a year. Your sponsorship can be in memory of or in honor of a person or animal.
Sponsor a Plaque or River Stone
Sponsor a plaque on one of our rooms, the welcome wall, our mural, or on a garden river stone. Your sponsorship can be in memory of or in honor of a person or animal.
Corporate Sponsor
Become a corporate sponsor for a fundraising event or donate items to our annual silent auction. Email us for more information.
Annual Gift
To make sure that we are here to help dogs left behind into the future, consider pledging an annual gift. We will contact you annually to renew your gift.
Legacy Donor
Legacy Donors are keystone supporters of our mission. Be an invaluable part of our mission with your annual gift of $5000 or more.
Planned Giving
Make a lasting gift to help animals in need. Planned giving can provide you with tax benefits while simultaneously helping animals.
Perpetual Care
Create a Pet Trust for your dog and make an annual gift of any amount to POMDR and we will commit to providing a loving, safe home for your dog in case something happens to you.
Donate Stock
Donate appreciated stock to POMDR and receive tax benefits. We are set up to receive your donations of stock. Email us for more information.
Wish List
Donate supplies to help our senior dogs. See what supplies our dogs need while in foster care.
