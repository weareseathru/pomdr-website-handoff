We Need Foster Homes!

We typically have about 80 dogs in our care at any given time. We also typically have up to 15 dogs waiting to get into foster homes.

POMDR provides supplies such as crate, bed, collar, harness, leash, id tag, bowls, toys, flea prevention. We also cover all medical expenses. We ask that our foster homes provide the food and a loving, safe home until your foster dog is adopted.

Fostering is a wonderful way to help dogs left behind find their forever homes. You may also want to foster as a way of finding the perfect match for your home if you are looking to add a dog to your family. Click here for more information about volunteering and fostering and to fill out an online volunteer questionnaire.

Some dogs in our program are very popular, and multiple foster volunteers may be interested in fostering the same one. If the dog you are interested in is no longer available, please be patient, many others still need a foster home. Please note that some dogs may have medical or behavioral needs, which will be disclosed (if known) when you inquire about them. Thank you for your patience.

Gabby
Looks like: Schnauzer, Poodle mix
Female, 13 years old (estimated), 37 pounds
Gorgeous Gabby may be enjoying her golden years, but this sweet senior Schnoodle still has plenty of pep in her step! She loves her daily walks, basking in the sunshine, and staying close to her people for gentle pets and affection. With her moderate energy level, Gabby is the perfect companion for someone who enjoys a leisurely stroll followed by a cozy cuddle session on the couch.

Gabby is as easygoing as they come. She gets along wonderfully with both dogs and cats, making her a great fit for many types of homes. She isn't demanding or overbearing - just a calm, loving presence with soulful eyes that have a way of capturing everyone's heart. Gabby is also delightfully quiet, usually only offering a bark or two every few days when she's hoping for a little extra attention.

One of Gabby's many wonderful qualities is that she is fully housetrained, according to her foster. In fact, she arrived without even a single accident, which is a rare and impressive testament to what a good girl she is.

Since arriving, Gabby has been receiving the medical care she needs to help her feel her best. She is currently being treated for Cushing's disease and is working toward losing approximately 9 pounds. Her weight loss journey will take time, even with reduced calories, and may require several months of patience and consistency. Reaching a healthier weight is an important step before she can safely undergo a dental cleaning and have a couple of lipomas removed.

Gabby is also an enthusiastic eater and has a habit of consuming things she shouldn't. Her future family will need to provide a safe environment by keeping potentially harmful items out of reach, including products such as snail or rat bait.

Gabby is a special senior girl who deserves a home where her health needs are understood and where she can spend her days feeling safe, loved, and cherished. If you believe life's sweetest companions only get better with age, Gabby is ready to show you just how wonderful a senior dog can be.

Gabby joined the POMDR pack after her loving guardian was no longer able to care for her.

Gabby needs to move fosters soon! Interested in meeting her? Please reach out so that you can come meet this precious girl and let her fill your home and heart with love.
