Who will care for your dog if you no longer can?

Our mission is to be a resource and advocate for senior dogs and senior people on California's Central Coast.
Intake & Adoptions
We find loving homes for dogs whose guardians can no longer care for them and for senior dogs in shelters.
Helping Paw
We provide volunteer and financial assistance so seniors can keep their pets with them as long as possible.
Perpetual Care
We make pre-arrangements to take in dogs should their guardians become unable to care for them.
