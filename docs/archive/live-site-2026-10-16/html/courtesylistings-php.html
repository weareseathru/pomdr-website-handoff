Other Adoptable Dogs - Courtesy Listings

The following dogs are not under the care of Peace of Mind Dog Rescue. We provide these listings as a courtesy to the community to help these dogs find new homes. Please contact the person named in the dog's writeup if you're interested in finding out more about one of them.

Mikki
Posted 10/16/2026
Mikki is truly a gentleman. At just 3 years old, he has a lot of love to give, but he knows how to keep it mellow and low-key. He is polite with other dogs, great with children, and good with cats--even if they put him in his place when he gets rambunctious. He does best when introduced to new dogs mindfully and slowly, but he can settle with them well!

Mikki joined POMDR after his former guardian developed dementia and could no longer care for him. After his guardian's children stepped up, they lost their home to a fire. Despite everything, Mikki has persevered and shown that even through hard times, one can have a kind heart. All he asks for is a safe place to call his own and a family to love unconditionally.

Bailey Bae
Updated 9/8/2026
Bailey is a beautiful, affectionate gal with the most loving big brown eyes, a smile that wins people over instantly, and with her playful energy, most people are shocked to hear she's 9. She loves daily walks, cuddle time, and being close to her person. She is 30 lbs. and working toward her ideal weight of around 24 lbs., where she'll truly be feeling her best, and naturally long-haired but groomed every few months.

You can find her on Instagram at @its_baileybae.

She is very smart and familiar with her basic commands, including sit, stay, come, leave it, shake (left and right!), and spin. Bailey loves 2 walks a day, but is happy with one solid outing -- whether that's a neighborhood walk, a hike (she can comfortably cover several miles), or a trip to the beach (she's not a water girl, which conveniently keeps her clean!). Bonus points if she spots a lizard to chase or gopher holes to investigate. And don't let her age fool you -- get her going with a squeaky toy and she plays like a puppy.

Bailey requires a child-free home where she can feel safe, relaxed, and fully herself. She is fearful and reactive around young children, so a home without them is a must. She also truly shines as the only pet -- no competing for attention, just all the love directed at her. She would thrive with someone (or a couple) who can give her plenty of company, whether that's a retiree, someone who works from home, or a person who can bring her along to work.

If you'd like to learn more about Bailey, please contact Michelle at (408) 250-4422

Obi
Updated 7/17/2026
Sweet Obi is a 7-year-old terrier mix, about 14 lbs. Obi is mostly a homebody. Mellow and quiet, he loves cozying up in blankets all day and enjoys snuggles and cuddles. Once he forms a connection, he becomes very lovey-dovey. Short walks or hikes in quiet areas are his favorite, especially when he gets to sniff everything at his own pace. He doesn't need much exercise and isn't a high-energy dog, so working from home with him close by is easy and distraction-free.

Food is a huge motivator for him. He's the kind of dog who won't refuse a meal, even when he's not feeling well. Kongs and rodent-shaped stuffed animals are his favorite toys. He knows all the main commands (sit, jump, come, wait, stay, relax, crate, etc.) and is fully house-trained. Although he has separation anxiety and can be protective of his parents, he's also been incredibly brave and smart, making real progress in facing his fears. He deserves the chance to keep healing and building confidence.

If you are interested in learning more about Obi, please contact Andrea at 669-240-6425 or by email at [email protected]
