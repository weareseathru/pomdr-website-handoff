Hatchi

Looks like: Siberian Husky
Male, 8 years old (estimated), 77 pounds
Handsome Hatchi the Husky is looking for his forever home. Could it be yours? This well-mannered fellow enjoys walks, romps with other pups, and spending time with his people. He is a good boy with adults and children, minding his manners for every meeting. He is a gentleman with other dogs and does not mind the presence of kitties, giving a polite sniff and then moving along. He especially loves giving kisses to people! Hatchi also does not seem to be anxious about being home without his people, but he sure is happy when they return, joyfully greeting them and showing his affection with his Husky song! He also enjoys riding in the car and makes sure to stick his head out the window to get the full experience!

Hatchi is a typical Husky in that he will need a securely fenced yard and will benefit from daily walks or trips to the dog park to get that playful energy out and allow him to safely explore. His ideal day might look something like a car ride to a fun hiking or walking adventure, followed by curling up on a soft blanket and settling in for the evening with his family.

This sweet boy came to us from Hitchcock Road Animal Services after being surrendered by his guardian. He was adopted and loved for a few years, but his adopter ultimately found that his intensive work schedule was not fair to Hatchi. Hatchi now continues his journey to find his loving forever home.
