### Helping Paw Program
Si no puede completar una solicitud o necesita más información, puede llamarnos al (831) 718-9122. Tenga en cuenta que esto retrasará el procesamiento de su solicitud.
[image: src=https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/images/tinapaw.jpg alt="Tina Helping Paw"]
Our Helping Paw program assists seniors, hospice patients and other pet guardians facing challenging times in keeping their beloved dogs and cats , providing a bridge that allows them stay together as long as possible.
When people are physically or financially unable to care for their pets, Peace of Mind Dog Rescue would like to lend a helping paw.
Our Helping Paw program provides both volunteer assistance and financial assistance.

#### Helping Paw Supplies
We can provide the following supplies that have been donated to us, as available. They must be picked up at our headquarters in Pacific Grove.
- Dog and Cat Food
- Flea prevention
- Collars
- Leashes
- Crates
- ID tags
- Blankets
- Dog jackets, sweaters, raincoats

#### Need Further Assistance?
If you are unable to fill out an application or need more information, you can call us at (831) 718-9122. Please be aware that this will delay the processing of your request.
