### Major Plaque/Stone Sponsorship Opportunities
POMDR has three special types of sponsorship opportunities in memory of a dog or a person or in honor of a dog or a person. Your sponsorship will help POMDR carry out our mission of helping senior dogs and senior people.
See all Plaque/Stone Sponsors
  (links: See all Plaque/Stone Sponsors -> https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/majorplaquestonesponsors.html)
[image: src=https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/images/welcomeroom.jpg alt=""]
Your 6 x 3.5 inch brushed gold sponsorship plaque will be displayed in the POMDR Welcome Room. There is room to engrave your name and the name of the person or dog you are honoring.
Become a Welcome Room Sponsor
  (links: Become a Welcome Room Sponsor -> https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/POMDRWelcomeRoomPlaqueSponsorship.php)
[image: src=https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/images/mural.jpg alt=""]
Your 4 inch engraved glass plaque will be displayed on the POMDR mural in Morgan’s Memorial Garden. There is room to engrave your name and the name of the person or dog you are honoring.
Become a Mural Sponsor.
  (links: Become a Mural Sponsor. -> https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/POMDRMuralPlaqueSponsorship.php)
[image: src=https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/images/gardenriverstone.jpg alt=""]
Your 8 - 10 inch personalized river stone will be placed in Morgan’s Memorial Garden at the POMDR Bauer Center. There is room to engrave the name of the dog or person you are honoring, and optional paw prints.
Become a Garden River Stone Sponsor.
  (links: Become a Garden River Stone Sponsor. -> https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/POMDRRiverStoneSponsorship.php)
