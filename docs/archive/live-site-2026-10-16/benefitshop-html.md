### Benefit Shop - Where Hidden Gems are Found
[image: src=https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/images/misc/donate/benefitshopmontage.jpg alt="Benefit Shop Montage"]
223 Grand Ave, Suite 1 Pacific Grove (831) 312-8991
Tuesday - Saturday 11:00 am - 5:00 pm
    (closed Sunday & Monday)
The POMDR Benefit Shop specializes in higher-end lightly used merchandise at reasonable prices.
If you are interested in volunteering at the Benefit Shop, please fill out an application .
  (links: fill out an application -> https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/POMDRVolunteerApplication.php)
Please come check out our art work, clothing, jewelry, home goods, furniture and other merchandise.
If you’d like to make a donation, contact [email protected] . Please let us know what you have to donate and your preferred day and time frame. Thank you!
  (links: [email protected] -> https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#34475c5b4474445b5950461a5b4653)

#### Items We Take
- Accessories such as jewelry, purses and scarves
- Home decor such as artwork and frames
- Lamps and lighting
- Diningware such as dish sets and glassware
- Patio decor
- Seasonal and holiday decor items (in season)
- Vintage and collectibles for the home
- Table Linens
- Dressers, book cases, coffee and end tables, chairs and kitchen/dining room tables.  (If your item is LARGE, please email a photo to [email protected] and our shop manager will get back to you to ensure we have space for your item.)
  (links: [email protected] -> https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#35465d5a4575455a5851471b5a4752)
- Books
- Gardening items
- Sports equipment – sports balls, hand weights, small equipment, etc.
- Toys and games
- Clothing - men's and women's lightly worn items

#### Items We Cannot Use
- Mattresses
- Baby equipment such as car seats and high chairs due to safety regulations and recall
- Upholstered furniture of any type
- Hangers
- Medical and mobility equipment such as a walkers and portable toilet seats
- TVs
- Toxic materials and already opened paint cans.
- Anything in disrepair, dirty, moldy or mildewed
