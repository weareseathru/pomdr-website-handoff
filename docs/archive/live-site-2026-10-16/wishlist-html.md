### Wish List
We have an average of 60 senior dogs in our care at any given time. Each foster dog needs a set of supplies to go with them to their foster home. We are always in need of the following items to help our foster homes care for the dogs:
- Small and Medium Crates (We have an abundance of large crates.)
- Collars (No buckle collars, choke chains or pinch collars please.)
- 4 or 6 foot Leashes (No flexi leashes)
- Harnesses
- Dog Beds
- Small Dog Blankets (No large blankets or comforters)
- Bowls
- Ex Pens
- Dog Gates
- Baby Gates
- Help Em Up Harnesses
- Citronella Bark Collars and Refills
- Coats
- Sweaters
- Flea Prevention
- Gift Cards from these stores: Pet Food Express, The Raw Connection, Diggidy Dog, Stone's Pet Shop, PetSmart, Petco, Earthwise, Wishbone Pet Co., Costco, and Target
- Check out our Amazon Wish List
  (links: Amazon Wish List -> http://a.co/iz8iJNF)
If you'd like to donate any items from this wish list, please bring them to the POMDR Bauer Center Monday through Saturday, 10am - 4pm. If the door is locked, ring the door bell.
Peace of Mind Dog Rescue Bauer Center 615 Forest Avenue Pacific Grove, CA 93950
If you have any questions or would like to provide funds to purchase items from the wish list, email us or call (831) 718-9122
  (links: email us -> https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#147d7a727b5464717577717b72797d7a70707b736671677761713a7b6673)
Please consider making donations for animals. They so appreciate your generosity!
