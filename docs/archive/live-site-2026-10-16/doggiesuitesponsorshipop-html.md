### Doggie Suite Sponsorship
Your sponsorship will help POMDR carry out our mission of helping senior dogs and senior people.
[image: src=https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/images/doggiesuite.jpg alt=""]
Your name will be displayed on the door of one of our doggie suites. The suites house the dogs who come into our program while they are getting their veterinary care completed to be ready for adoption. There is room to include the name of a person or dog you are honoring as well.
[link: Sponsor a Doggie Suite -> https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/POMDRDoggieSuiteSponsorship.php]
