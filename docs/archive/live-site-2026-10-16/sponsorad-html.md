### Sponsor an Ad
If you or your business would like to sponsor an ad in the Carmel Pine Cone, Monterey County Weekly, the Monterey County Herald, or the Santa Cruz Good Times Weekly, please email us . We welcome sponsors who would like to sponsor a one-time ad or are interested in sponsoring one ad per month ongoing. The cost of each ad is $150.
  (links: email us -> https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#51383f373e1121343032343e373c383f35353e362334223224347f3e2336)
We also welcome sponsors for radio spots on NPR stations KAZU and KUSP. NPR sponsorships cost a minimum or $1000 and are a great way to spread the word about our work.
