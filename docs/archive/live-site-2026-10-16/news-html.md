# POMDR What’s Happening

**Meta description:** Peace of Mind Dog Rescue is dedicated to finding new loving homes for dogs whose guardians can no longer care for them due to illness, death, or other challenging circumstances, and to finding homes for senior dogs in animal shelters.

#### Free Pet Trust Seminar
[image: src=https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/images/webemails/uploads/Pet Trust Seminar Cover.jpeg alt="Free"]
Time: 10a - Noon
Where: Via Zoom
What: Learn how a pet trust can bring you peace of mind and ensure your furry family members are cared for in the event of your death or disability. Attorney Whitney Clark will answer your questions about setting up a pet trust for your special four-legged friends. You'll also learn more about our Perpetual Care Program .
