# Peace of Mind Dog Rescue Home
**Address:** https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/index.php  
**Meta description:** Peace of Mind Dog Rescue is dedicated to finding new loving homes for dogs whose guardians can no longer care for them due to illness, death, or other challenging circumstances, and to finding homes for senior dogs in animal shelters.
