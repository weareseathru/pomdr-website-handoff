### POMDR Harry and Jaynne Boand Clinic
[image: src=https://www.peaceofminddogrescue.org/images/misc/BoandClinic.jpg alt="POMDR Boand clinic"]
The POMDR Boand Clinic is located at 1251 10th Street in Monterey. Opened in November 2019, the clinic provides medical care for the dogs in our program. Every dog we bring into our program gets a full veterinary exam, senior blood panel, heartworm test, urinalysis, vaccine and microchips.
Most of the dogs also need a dental cleaning and many need extractions. We also address any lumps that need to be aspirated, biopsied or removed. We have a full x-ray machine, dental x-ray machine, and ultrasound.
We refer to veterinary specialists as needed including ophthalmology, oncology, dermatology, dentistry and internal medicine doctors.
