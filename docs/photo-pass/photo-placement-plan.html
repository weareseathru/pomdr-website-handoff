Photo Placement Plan

Where each photo goes, why, and the evidence for it. Prepared 12 November 2026 for Andrew Z. Nothing here has been changed on the site yet.

The plan in one look

The rule you set: do not change what already works. So the first move is to keep the photos you like and make them sharp. A photo is only replaced where it is too small and no original could be found.

How each choice is proved

1. Measured, not judged

Every photo's pixel width is compared with the width it fills on a laptop. Under 1× is too small, 1× to 1.6× is soft on phones and new laptops, above that is sharp. The site stores photos at 2,000 px at most, so the planned figure is capped there.

2. Shown, not described

Each planned photo appears cropped to the exact shape of its spot, next to what is there today, with the share of the photo that survives the crop.

3. Scored against your guide

Replacements are scored out of 10 on the draft screening guide: feeling, light, framing, POMDR in view, and story. The guide is still waiting for your approval, so treat the scores as provisional.
